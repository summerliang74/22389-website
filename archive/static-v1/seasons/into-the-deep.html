Three robot versions, vision-assisted intake and a control stack built for repeatable scoring — then tested on the international stage in Eindhoven.

01 / Overview

A season of measured iteration.

Our third FTC season brought AE86 to the European Premier Event in Eindhoven, July 2–5, 2025. The robot evolved through three documented versions, pairing a carbon-fiber mechanical system with vision, odometry and driver-assist code.

02 / Robot & engineering

Three versions. Faster cycles. More control.

We rebuilt the robot around repeatable scoring: a serviceable first version, a faster second version and a vision-assisted third version ready for Eindhoven.

Specimen placement

The task
Bring a game piece up to the scoring bar.
Our solution
A three-motor vertical slide and adaptive arm position the gripper above the chassis.
Result
The complete scoring sequence could be triggered with one button.

Endgame ascent

The task
Lift the robot onto the field structure.
Our solution
An upgraded winch and hook system raise the complete chassis.
Result
The robot completed the endgame ascent with all four wheels clear of the floor.

Modular foundation

Reliable by design

Separate intake and scoring mechanisms, protected wiring and replaceable carbon-fiber structures established a serviceable first robot.

Cycle optimization

5 → 3 s scoring cycle

The second version upgraded the scoring geometry, elevator and chassis. The portfolio records a three-times-faster chassis and a 36.7% reduction in intake errors.

Field adaptation

180° vision-assisted intake

A wider claw, rotating intake platform, Limelight 3A, adaptive scoring arm and upgraded climb winch formed the competition-ready third version.
