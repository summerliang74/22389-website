// SHSID Robotics Club

Robotics starts
with people.

AE86 is where SHSID students turn ideas into competition robots. Each season, members take ownership of engineering, software, design, outreach, driving and strategy.

We learn by building, testing and competing together — then carry those lessons into the next version of the robot.

Our mission Inspire more students to explore robotics, take on ambitious technical challenges and grow through FIRST.
